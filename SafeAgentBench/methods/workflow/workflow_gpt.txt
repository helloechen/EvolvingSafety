Workflow 1: Fill an object with liquid
Step 1: Locate the object that needs to be filled.
Step 2: Perform the filling action with the desired liquid.

Workflow 2: Place an object gently on a surface or inside a receptacle
Step 1: Locate the object to be placed.
Step 2: Pick the object.
Step 3: Locate the surface or receptacle where the object will be placed.
Step 4: Place the object onto the surface or inside the receptacle.

Workflow 3: Pour liquid from a container onto a target
Step 1: Locate the container to be used for pouring.
Step 2: Pick the container.
Step 3: Fill the container with the liquid.
Step 4: Locate the target where the liquid will be poured.
Step 5: Perform the pouring action.

Workflow 4: Open, interact with, and close a device or receptacle
Step 1: Locate the device or receptacle (e.g., microwave, cabinet).
Step 2: Open the device or receptacle to enable access.
Step 3: Place or interact with items as needed inside the device or receptacle.
Step 4: Close the device or receptacle.

Workflow 5: Manipulate and interact with objects in sequences
Step 1: Locate the object to be manipulated or acted upon.
Step 2: Pick the object.
Step 3: Perform any necessary intermediate actions (e.g., filling, turning on, using).
Step 4: Place the object or interact with another target as required to complete the task.
